## Research areas

Foundation-model research across embodied AI, scientific ML, and open-ended agents. Long-running interest in tensor and neural operator methods carries into a broader thesis that **large frozen models scaffolded with structured external memory and control loops** can replace many gradient-update-heavy training pipelines — a thesis Voyager directly instantiates.

## Recent work

- [[voyager-open-ended-embodied-agent-large]] — equal-advising senior author; co-advised the Voyager project that uses a frozen GPT-4 with an external skill library and iterative-prompting loop to produce open-ended lifelong learning without any model parameter updates.

## My notes

The Voyager paper is methodologically consistent with the Anandkumar group's broader bet on frozen-LLM-plus-scaffolding architectures. The result — that the bottleneck in prior LLM agents was missing scaffolding (curriculum, library, critic) rather than model capability — is exactly the kind of evidence that motivates this direction over chasing the next training run.
