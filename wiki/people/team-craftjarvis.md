## Research areas

Team CraftJarvis is a cross-institution research group spanning Peking University, UCLA, Beijing University of Posts and Telecommunications, and the Beijing Institute for General Artificial Intelligence. Their public research line treats Minecraft as a sustained benchmark for open-world generalist agents, releasing successive papers on situation-aware planning, multimodal memory, instruction-following controllers, and the interfaces between high-level LLM planners and low-level policies.

## Recent work

- [[jarvis-open-world-multi-task-agents]] — team byline; introduced JARVIS-1, a memory-augmented multimodal-language-model agent for Minecraft.
