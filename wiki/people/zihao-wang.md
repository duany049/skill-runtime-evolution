## Research areas

Open-world embodied agents, with a focus on Minecraft as a testbed for long-horizon planning and lifelong learning. Specializes in chaining multimodal foundation models (MineCLIP) with LLM planners, equipping them with experience-based memory, and using self-instruct curricula to drive autonomous capability growth.

## Recent work

- [[jarvis-open-world-multi-task-agents]] — first author; introduced JARVIS-1, a memory-augmented multimodal-language-model agent that achieves 5x reliability over VPT on long-horizon diamond-pickaxe tasks in Minecraft.

## My notes

Part of Team CraftJarvis at Peking University. The CraftJarvis line of work treats Minecraft as a sustained benchmark for open-world generalist agents, with successive papers exploring different angles (situation-aware planning, multimodal memory, controller-policy interfaces).
