## Research areas

LLM-driven embodied lifelong-learning agents, with Minecraft as the testbed for open-ended exploration. Lead author on Voyager, the first GPT-4-powered agent to combine an automatic curriculum, an executable skill library, and an iterative code-refinement loop with self-verification.

## Recent work

- [[voyager-open-ended-embodied-agent-large]] — first author; introduced Voyager, the first LLM-powered embodied lifelong learning agent in Minecraft, outperforming prior SOTA by 3.3x on unique items and being the only baseline to unlock the diamond tier.

## My notes

Joint NVIDIA / Caltech affiliation puts Wang at the intersection of industrial LLM deployment (GPT-4 black-box API workflows) and academic open-ended-exploration research. The decision to drive Voyager entirely through prompting — no fine-tuning, no gradient updates — reads as a deliberate bet that scaffolding around a frozen LLM yields more progress than tuning a smaller one.
