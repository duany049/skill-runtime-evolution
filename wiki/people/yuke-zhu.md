Robot learning and embodied AI — long-horizon manipulation, imitation learning from large-scale demonstrations, and benchmarks (robosuite). Joint NVIDIA / UT Austin appointment bridges industrial robotics research and academic embodied-AI work. Co-author on Voyager, contributing the embodied-AI and skill-composition perspective that shapes the executable skill library's design.

## Recent work

## My notes

Zhu's prior robosuite work on benchmark design for manipulation skills sits behind Voyager's commitment to *programs* (rather than primitives) as the action space — the choice that makes skill composition possible. His broader research line treats reusable embodied skills as the unit of learning, which is exactly what Voyager's executable skill library operationalizes for LLM-driven agents.
